What to expect

Volunteering with KRBS is rewarding, challenging, and sometimes intense. There are various roles including phone coordination and on-site rescue work. Whatever role you perform, you will be helping to protect the lives of koalas across Brisbane south.

We are looking for people who are: mentally prepared for emergencies; focused on teamwork; willing to learn; committed to the mission; passionate about koalas; and for some of the on-site rescue roles, physically capable for the more strenuous activities. You don't need prior experience.

Volunteer roles

There's a place for everyone, whether you're on the front line or behind the scenes.

phone_in_talk

Phone coordinator

Respond to calls from the public 24/7 — choose your shift. Record details, organise a first responder to attend and liaise with the rescue lead.

emergency

First responder

Be the first KRBS volunteer on scene. Talk with the member of the public, assess the situation, tag the location, and brief the rescue lead.

groups

Rescuer / rescue support

Rescue activities include spotting, netting, catching, directing the public and pole handling. Rescue supporters choose their level of activity.

timer

Trap / tree sitter

Quietly monitor koalas in risky locations or those that need containing, in one or two hour blocks. Sometimes regular drive-by check-ins are needed instead.

directions_car

Transporter

Drive a rescued koala to veterinary care or a safe release location. Requires your own vehicle and availability on a needs basis.

admin_panel_settings

Administration

Sub-roles include: Office Bearer, Membership Registrar, organising training, updating records and documents, general technology, and making signs.

campaign

Community advocate

Spread awareness at events, contribute to social media, and organise or assist at fundraisers. Help people care about koalas before a crisis hits.

Training & support

Every volunteer receives the necessary training, which might cover rescue protocols, animal handling, safety procedures, communication and wildlife science. We meet regularly for hands-on training and team development.

John Knights mentors new volunteers. You'll learn from someone who has been rescuing koalas for over 15 years, and you'll be equipped with the skills and confidence to respond safely and appropriately in any situation.

schedule Flexible commitment. We know life is busy. We work around your schedule and ask only that you show up when you say you will.

KRBS

Koala Rescue Brisbane South is a community-driven rescue organisation, founded and mentored by John Knights, who has over 15 years of koala expertise. We respond 24/7 to koalas in need across Brisbane's southern suburbs.
