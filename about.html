John's vision

John Knights has spent over 15 years in wildlife rescue. Holding a permit from the Queensland Department of Environment, Tourism, Science and Innovation (DETSI) for koala rescue, he founded Koala Rescue Brisbane South Inc. with a simple belief: every koala in need deserves professional, compassionate care, no matter the time, the weather, or how hard it is to reach it.

What started as one person with a mission has grown into something remarkable. A growing team of dedicated local volunteers, each bringing unique skills and genuine passion, now responds to koalas in need across Brisbane south.

John mentors every volunteer. He believes the best rescuers are made, not found. He's committed to building a team that will outlast any single individual.

How we work

When someone calls, we respond — 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Trained volunteers arrive with the right equipment, the right knowledge, and the right attitude to get the job done safely.

But we don't just rescue. We assess, we relocate when necessary, and we reunite animals with their habitat after a stay in the wildlife hospital. Every rescue is documented. Every case is a chance to help protect our koalas.

The people behind the mission

Our volunteers perform roles such as: phone coordinators, first responders, rescuers, rescue support, trap sitters, transporters, tree climbers, administration, and of course, community advocates. They're not just titles; they're local people who care.

From the person who spots a koala in need on their morning walk to the rescuer who drives out at midnight, everyone plays a vital role. We celebrate each other, support each other, and make decisions together.

We communicate regularly, ensuring that we: stay sharp, stay connected, and stay ready.
